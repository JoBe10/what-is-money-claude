# Act II — The Provenance Map
## Every frame of Scenes 5–10, classified against the legacy deck

**Status: RULED, 31 August 2026.** The presenter ruled every row in one pass. **This document now governs all Act II work**, and every Act II session verifies a frame's class here before touching it (`AGENTS.md` §4.9).
**Scope:** the fourteen style frames of `docs/batch-b-package.md` §3, ◆ and non-◆ alike.
**Classes:** **PORT** — a proven legacy treatment exists and is transplanted verbatim; redesign is forbidden and generating candidates is a process defect. **ADAPT** — a proven treatment with one ruled change, named here; only that change is made. **NEW** — no proven treatment exists; the full-coverage rule and the candidate process apply.
**Proposed:** 30 August 2026. **Ruled:** 31 August 2026.

# 0. The convention this map classifies by

> **A frame is classified by its treatment — not by its subject and not by its words.** Putting a different render or a different sentence through a proven treatment does not make the frame new. Changing *how the frame is made* does. Where the architecture ruled a change to the treatment itself, that ruling is the frame's ADAPT ruling.

**RULED 31 August 2026 — the answer was *treatment*.** The convention stands exactly as proposed; it was confirmed rather than narrowed. That single answer settles four rows: **S6-F1, S7-F1, S8-F1 and S9-F2 are PORT.** Scene 7's vault study is therefore a PORT of an approved study box even though no vault has ever been on screen, because a subject is not a treatment — and any later session tempted to read an unfamiliar picture as a new frame is answered here rather than row by row.

# 2. The four rows that were ARGUABLE — how they were ruled

The two sides each row was weighed on stay on file: a ruling is worth more later when the thing it decided against is still legible.

## S5-F1 ◆ — the through-line composition · **RULED PORT**

> **The ruling:** `4-06`'s `ClaimObject`-in-`CarrierShell`, construction C — with the presenter's carrier-centering ruling already in the component. **The photographic renders remain the historical objects in the same scenes.**

That last clause is what dissolved the objection. The proposal read the frame as forcing a choice between a line-grammar carrier and a photographic one; the ruling says the scene carries both, in their own registers and their own moments — the claim wears the drawn carrier, and the dark-field renders are the historical goods those scenes also show. Master §6.3's boundary is kept, not crossed.

- *Weighed for PORT:* the composition exists and was already judged — `CarrierShell`'s construction was drawn three ways at shipping scale and selected (C, the banded wall, R7.2) — and Batch B's own spec names that object.
- *Weighed for NEW:* Batch B's carrier is the `shells` render, and master §6.3 forbids a render inside a diagram, so a photographic carrier around a line-grammar claim had no proven answer.

## S5-F3 ◆ — the Zanzibar receipt · **RULED ADAPT**

> **The ruling:** `2-07`'s dated-fact treatment, with one change: ***generalized as the film-wide evidence grammar*** — Zanzibar, 1971 and 1803 all speak it.

The proposal's two sides were both right about their halves, and the ruling takes both: the treatment is proven, and what was unproven was only its generality. So the treatment ports and the generalization is the one named change — which is exactly what ADAPT is for.

- *Weighed for PORT:* `2-07` lands **`1971.`** alone at display scale with one decree sentence beneath, and the Zanzibar receipt already exists as the `receipt` wound in `2-04` build 7.
- *Weighed for NEW:* both proven forms are attached to a rail component the architecture retired, and a system that must carry place·date·fact and date·fact alike had never been proven as a system.

**Consequence:** the systems sheet's three S5-F3 candidates **do not apply** — the frame is not NEW.

## S7-F2 — the detachment · **RULED NEW**

> **The ruling:** the legacy detaching-rung's meaning is **vertical abstraction**; the film's moment is **outward travel with a dependency line back**.

The ruling is sharper than the proposal's NEW side and worth quoting for that reason: the objection was that the severance *cuts* where Scene 7 *holds*, but the ruling names the deeper difference — the legacy gesture moves *up a ladder of abstraction*, and Scene 7's claim moves *out across distance*. Those are different drawings, not one drawing with a parameter changed.

- *Weighed for ADAPT:* `2-07` already draws a claim leaving its base and settling apart from it.
- *Weighed for NEW:* the severance's whole argument is the tether breaking, and Scene 7's is the tether holding.

**Consequence:** candidates are required, for **the travel's geometry**.

## S8-F2 ◆ — the four-currency chart · **RULED PORT**

> **The ruling:** the legacy chart untouched, frozen data and all. **An ADAPT is available later only if the presenter's in-context viewing rules a clash.**

- *Weighed for PORT:* `2-07` already renders it and every judgment in it is made — one vertex per observed year (*"the line is the record, not a curve fit"*), per-series alpha carrying the emphasis, the 1971 = 100 reference line, the end-label spread, the index note.
- *Weighed for ADAPT:* Batch B says *at film grade*, and Scene 8's chart stands alone where the legacy's stood over a dimmed rail.

**Consequence:** the systems sheet's three S8-F2 candidates **do not apply**. The reservation is recorded rather than exercised: if the chart clashes with its neighbours when he watches Scene 8 in sequence, the ADAPT is available then — and it would be a new ruling, not a reopening of this one.

# 7. How this map governs

Every Act II session, before touching a frame:

1. **Look the frame up here.** A frame with no row is unclassified, and classifying it is the presenter's.
2. **PORT** — transplant the named treatment verbatim. Grade, type and stage wiring only. **A port that cannot reproduce its treatment without a design decision is a stop-and-flag, not a judgment call.**
3. **ADAPT** — make exactly the one named change. Everything else is a port.
4. **NEW** — the full-coverage rule and the candidate process (§4.3, §4.5), reusing this act's existing candidates where §6 says they apply rather than generating parallel ones.

Amendments are presenter rulings only. The one reservation on file is S8-F2's (§2): an ADAPT is available if his in-context viewing rules a clash, and that would be a new ruling rather than a reopening.
