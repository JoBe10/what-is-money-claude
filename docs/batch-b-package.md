**S9 must contain (neutrality at maximum):** description, never argument; the facts — 2009, no state, no company, 21,000,000 units fixed by the protocol's rules, holdable directly like a bearer asset; the three capabilities; **the honesty in the same breath** — very young, price swings far more than the monies it would compete with, not yet twenty years into a hundred-year question; the distributed stability content (Ruling 5): the two-question distinction (the market's valuation of the asset vs the architecture of the claim) and volatility as a stage, not a verdict — with reduction an expectation, never a guarantee. **S10 must contain:** one gain and one dependency per architecture on the strip; the history line; **palladium as the bar** — scarcer in supply than gold, at times pricier, discovered 1803 against a network millennia old, never money, never the reserve; the insufficiency line: *"Marginally better is structurally insufficient. Only a categorical difference on the deciding properties has ever moved the crown."*; the pivot question that opens Act III: better for what job?
